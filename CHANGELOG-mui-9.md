- Upgraded Material UI from v7 to v9 (v8 was skipped upstream), along with `@mui/icons-material`, `@mui/system`, `@mui/lab` (now a v9 beta) and `@mui/x-date-pickers` (v8 to v9). The bulk of the change is mechanical and was produced by MUI's own codemods: system props like `mt`, `flexGrow` and `alignItems` are no longer accepted directly on `Box`, `Stack`, `Typography`, `Link` and `Grid` and have moved into each component's `sx` prop across roughly 200 files, and the deprecated `inputProps`, `InputProps`, `ListboxComponent` and `renderTags` props have been replaced by their `slots`/`slotProps` equivalents. Local components that wrap those MUI primitives had their call sites converted the same way, and a shared `mergeSx` helper in `js/helpers/styled` merges a caller's `sx` into a component's own styles. Two behavioral notes: the transfer list on the LineUp page now uses `Stack` where it previously relied on `Grid` with `direction="column"`, which v9 removed, and `ListItemIcon`'s default minimum width drops from 56px to 36px, which tightens the spacing of icon lists site-wide. The v9 browser baseline also rises to Chrome 117, Firefox 121 and Safari 17.
