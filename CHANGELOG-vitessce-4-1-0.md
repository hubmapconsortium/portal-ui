- Upgraded Vitessce from 4.0.6 to 4.1.0, the first release built on MUI 9. Vitessce previously declared `@mui/material@^7` and pulled in MUI 7, `@mui/lab` 7 and an MUI X Data Grid 8 beta, which Vite's dedupe then forced onto the portal's MUI 9 at runtime. 4.1.0 depends on MUI 9 and Data Grid 9 and drops `@mui/lab`, so the lockfile now holds a single MUI major and Vitessce runs against the version it was built for.
